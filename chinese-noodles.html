Easy Chinese Fried Noodles

Description

A quick, savory, and comforting stir-fried noodle dish. This recipe features chewy egg noodles tossed with crisp vegetables and a rich, Umami-filled savory soy sauce blend that takes under 20 minutes to make.

Ingredients

  • 8 oz Chinese egg noodles (or ramen noodles)
  • 2 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 1 tablespoon dark soy sauce
  • 1 tablespoon sesame oil
  • 1 cup shredded cabbage
  • 1 large carrot, julienned
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 green onions, chopped

Steps

  1. Boil the noodles according to the package instructions, drain, and set aside.
  2. In a small bowl, whisk together the soy sauce, dark soy sauce, and sesame oil.
  3. Heat a large skillet or wok over high heat and add a splash of cooking oil.
  4. Sauté the minced garlic, shredded cabbage, and carrots for 2-3 minutes until slightly tender.
  5. Add the cooked noodles and the sauce mixture into the skillet.
  6. Toss everything together rapidly for 2 minutes until the noodles are evenly coated and steaming hot.
  7. Garnish with chopped green onions and serve immediately.
