Classic Kung Pao Chicken

Description

Kung Pao Chicken is a highly popular Szechuan stir-fry dish made with juicy chicken cubes, crispy peanuts, and fiery dried red chilies. It features a perfect balance of spicy, sweet, and tangy flavors brought together by a rich, glossy glaze.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb chicken breast or thighs, cut into bite-sized cubes
  • 1/2 cup unsalted roasted peanuts
  • 10-12 dried Szechuan red chilies, cut into pieces
  • 1 tablespoon Sichuan peppercorns
  • 1 bell pepper, chopped into squares
  • 2 cloves garlic, sliced
  • 1 teaspoon minced ginger
  • 2 tablespoons soy sauce (divided for marinade and sauce)
  • 1 tablespoon Chinese black vinegar (Chinkiang vinegar)
  • 1 tablespoon hoisin sauce
  • 1 teaspoon cornstarch

Steps

  1. Marinate the chicken cubes with one tablespoon of soy sauce and the cornstarch for 10 minutes.
  2. In a small bowl, whisk together the remaining soy sauce, black vinegar, hoisin sauce, and a splash of water to make the stir-fry sauce.
  3. Heat a tablespoon of oil in a wok or large skillet over high heat. Add the marinated chicken and sear until cooked through, then remove and set aside.
  4. Add another splash of oil to the wok. Reduce heat to medium and stir-fry the dried chilies and Szechuan peppercorns until fragrant, being careful not to burn them.
  5. Toss in the garlic, ginger, and chopped bell pepper. Stir-fry for 1-2 minutes until the vegetables are slightly tender.
  6. Return the chicken to the wok and pour in the prepared stir-fry sauce. Toss rapidly on high heat until the sauce thickens and coats everything.
  7. Stir in the roasted peanuts, remove from heat, and serve hot with steamed rice.
